What to import

A regular expression on the block's id and name. Only plain structural blocks (TypeId CubeBlock) that fit in one cell are imported. Armour with a game shape joins the matching vanilla card. Other blocks are read from their model and grouped by shape, so blocks with the same shape share one card.
Blocks larger than one cell (most wings) are counted but not imported yet.

Draw

Click two points to draw an edge of the block. Drag to orbit, right-drag to pan, wheel to zoom. The orange arrow is the block's Forward. Draw every edge of the shape, then Save.
Click a point to start a line.

Block

Saving turns the lines into a solid. If the lines close into a solid (each edge shared by two faces) that exact shape is used, concave or not. If not, the shape becomes the convex hull of the points and you get a warning.

Scale and grid

A block is considered for a cell only when the model fills at least this share of the block's own volume (a plate: of the model's thin skin). Among those, the winner is the block whose faces best match the model's surface in the cell: the surface should lie on the block's faces, and every face the block shows should have model surface on it. Simpler blocks take near-ties. Thin bits of a neighbour poking through a grid line get no block.
